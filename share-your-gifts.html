The first witness

It starts with one person willing to say it out loud

This circle was founded by Anthony Jossue Mora Mendez — sun369gazer — who has practiced sungazing for four years and describes himself as a son of the sun: the sun is his father, the earth his mother, and the stars his family.

☉ Sun & Light · Sight Beyond Sight · Dreams

“When I gaze at the sun, I see myself — my willingness to create.”

Anthony gazes at the sun for hours at a time and records what happens. He describes a blue filter coming on and covering the sun, the sun looking clearly round, a tingly, filled-up feeling, and “rainbows on steroids.” Afterward, he says, colors look brighter, his senses feel boosted for five to ten minutes, and his mind processes information faster — like going, in his words, from 56k dial-up to 100gb.

He says the sun speaks to him, saying something different every time, and that the afterglow when he closes his eyes is like playdough he can shape to sync the left and right sides of his brain — one side sharp lines, the other round shapes.

Left eye — the sun eyeWarmer, around 4500K. It is easier for him to see the sun directly with this eye alone.
Right eye — the moon eyeColder, around 6000K. Looking at the moon with this eye alone, he sees a blue and cyan-like halo around it.
In dreamsHe flies, casts spells, steps through portals, and sees things before he sees them awake — and calls that how he controls his reality.
Beyond the spectrumHe says spirits have shown him colors past the human visible spectrum, and that he can leave his body when he sleeps.
Do not judge a sun gazer for what you cannot do yourself. If someone cannot see the sun, they should listen to those who can — whose wisdom has reached a level where sight is just a pleasure to be enjoyed.

That is the rule this whole circle runs on, and it was the first piece Anthony chose to leave here for everyone who finds this page.

The circle’s way

How we hold each other’s stories

Anthony has been called on, dismissed, and misunderstood for what he can do — by strangers, and by family. This circle exists so that doesn’t have to be the pattern here.

1

Listen first

Hear the whole testimony before you form a single thought about it. Thought patterns are how we recognize each other.

2

Witness, don’t copy

No one here is asked to do what another person does, the way they do it. Witness the experience, then form your own opinion and your own path.

3

No judgment for what you can’t do

Not being able to gaze, fly, or see it yourself is not a reason to judge the person who can. Ignorance is no excuse to be rude.

4

Your experience, your words

Names, details, and meanings stay as the person who lived them tells them. Nobody rewrites another person’s gift into their own beliefs.

Questions people ask

Before you share

Honest answers, in the spirit of the circle.

Do I need proof of my gift?

No. This is a witness circle, not a courtroom. You tell what you experience, in your words, and others listen first. A photo, a piece of code, a recording — like Anthony’s telescope photo and his “Who I Am” code — can help people feel it, but the testimony itself is the gift.

Do I have to do it the way the founder does?

No — and he would be the first to say so. He doesn’t want people to do what he does, like him. He wants people to witness his experience and form their own opinions. Your gift is yours; this page just gives it a place to stand.

What if nobody around me understands?

Then you are exactly who this was built for. Anthony has said it is not easy being the only one who does something, in dreams or in the waking world — and that it would be easier to blend in, but that is not him. Here, being the only one is how every story on the wall starts.

How do I keep my testimony permanently?

Use the Download button to keep the file, or post it as a public GitHub Gist — free, permanent, and under your own username, the same way Anthony’s code and photo are posted under sun369gazer. From there you can make a QR code for it and share it anywhere.

Is there a cost?

No. Sharing your testimony here costs nothing, a Gist costs nothing, and keeping your downloaded file costs nothing. A custom domain name for your own site is the only optional extra, and you don’t need one to be witnessed.
